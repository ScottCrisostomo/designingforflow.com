Ubisoft 3rd Party Publishing — 2011–2014

3rd Party Publishing

Embedded Onsite Design Director — Multiple titles

Within Ubisoft's 3rd Party Publishing division, I served as an embedded external design director — traveling onsite to partner studios, conducting in-person build reviews, and providing actionable design and production guidance to help titles meet Ubisoft's quality standards and ship on schedule.

The role combined creative assessment, design direction, and production coordination. I reported findings directly to publishing leadership and executive producers, with my assessments feeding into milestone decisions and go/no-go calls. Where a title needed more than assessment, I could move into active intervention — embedding through late-stage development to close quality gaps and stabilize launches.

// the role

What embedded design direction actually involves

Being Ubisoft's eyes on the ground at an external studio is a specific kind of work. You land at a studio that has its own culture, its own creative director, and its own way of doing things — and your job is to help them make a better game, not impose a different one.

The practical work: reviewing builds against Ubisoft's quality bar, writing actionable reports that went directly to publishing leadership, facilitating conversations between the studio and Ubisoft's business development, legal, and marketing teams, and — when a title needed it — rolling up your sleeves and helping fix problems rather than just documenting them.

The skill it builds: reading a game's problems quickly, communicating findings clearly to both creative teams and executives, and building enough trust with an external studio to actually influence their decisions. Titles that got the most value from embedded direction were ones where the studio saw me as a resource, not an auditor.
