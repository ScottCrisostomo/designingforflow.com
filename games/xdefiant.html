Ubisoft – 2024

XDefiant

Associate Game Director – Feb 2021 to Dec 2024 – PC / PS4 / PS5 / Xbox One / Xbox Series X|S

// overview

Ubisoft's largest live-service launch

XDefiant was a free-to-play arena shooter built around factions drawn from Ubisoft's IP library – Watch Dogs, Ghost Recon, Splinter Cell, and others – each with distinct abilities and playstyles. It launched in 2024 as Ubisoft's most ambitious live-service title and reached 15 million players within its first weeks.

As Associate Game Director, I owned gameplay direction from early prototype through live operations – responsible for how the game felt to play, how it grew each season, and how the team across four global studios stayed aligned on that vision.

// what I designed

Design contributions

What are the 3Cs? Character, Camera, and Controls – the foundational layer of how a player's body moves through the game world. Getting the 3Cs right means the game feels good before a single enemy appears. Getting them wrong means no amount of content or balance work fixes it. On XDefiant, establishing the 3Cs was the first and most critical design challenge.
  • 3Cs and core prototype I personally built XDefiant's core FPS mechanics in Snowdrop to approximately 80% completion before handing off to senior designers for tuning and polish. Character movement, camera behavior, weapon feel, and moment-to-moment shooting were all prototyped in-engine first, giving the team a working reference rather than a document to interpret.
  • Seasonal roadmaps and live content direction Owned the seasonal content cadence across the game's live run – directing new maps, new factions, new weapons, new abilities, and gameplay modes each season. Each season ran on a roughly three-month cycle, requiring simultaneous planning, production, and live ops across multiple content streams.
  • Process design and approvals Built the design review and approval pipeline from the ground up – spec templates, review cadences, greenlight criteria, and escalation paths. On a game with distributed teams across San Francisco, Sydney, Osaka, and Saguenay, having clear processes wasn't overhead. It was what kept content quality consistent across studios.
  • Distributed team coordination Managed design delivery across four global studios, building cross-site communication rituals and alignment processes that kept all locations working against the same creative direction. San Francisco was the lead studio; the challenge was making every other studio feel like a genuine partner rather than a satellite.
  • New features: gameplay modes and bots Directed the design and production of new gameplay modes and the bot system – features that expanded how players could engage with XDefiant beyond its core competitive loop.
  • Progression and battle pass Owned battle pass structure, goal-based progression, and limited-time event design – tuning reward loops and player motivation systems across multiple live seasons using telemetry and player behavior data.
